# Content Policy and Production Guide

## Allowed source types

- `official_reference`
- `original_internal`
- `licensed_partner`

## Prohibited

Do not scrape or copy:
- Ruangguru
- Cerebrum
- Skuling
- commercial books/bimbel content without license
- other competitor banks

Official TKA material is used as blueprint/reference subject to applicable usage terms.

The long-term bank should be predominantly original internal content.

## Taxonomy-first rule

Do not mass-produce questions before the official Math taxonomy is reviewed and stable.

Hierarchy:

```
Subject
→ Topic
→ Subtopic
→ Skill
→ Question
```

## Publish gate

A question cannot be published without:

- subject
- topic
- subtopic
- at least one skill
- difficulty
- question type
- valid content
- correct answer
- explanation
- source type
- estimated time
- reviewer approval

Visual questions also require meaningful alt text.

## Content lifecycle

```
DRAFT
  ↓
IN_REVIEW
  ↓
APPROVED
  ↓
PUBLISHED
  ↓
ARCHIVED
```

Editor and reviewer responsibilities should be separate where practical.

## Math production strategy

Stage A: 30–50 representative questions.

Purpose:
- validate schema,
- validate renderer,
- validate CMS,
- validate mobile layouts.

Must cover:
- plain text
- LaTeX
- image
- table
- chart
- function graph
- single choice
- multiple choice

Stage B: 100+ questions.

Purpose:
- validate practice/mastery/adaptive behavior.

Math beta:
- approximately 200–300 reviewed questions,
- balanced by taxonomy coverage and difficulty.

Coverage and quality are more important than raw count.

## Explanation standard

Prefer:
- concise correct-answer statement,
- clear reasoning/steps,
- quick method when genuinely useful,
- common mistake,
- solving tip.

Do not pad explanations just to make them longer.

## Generated/template drills

Rule-based parameterized drills may be used selectively for basic skills, but should not dominate the bank. Avoid repetitive near-duplicates.
